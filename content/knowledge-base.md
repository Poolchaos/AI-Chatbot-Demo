**FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

*Booking & Process*

Q: How do I book a corporate retreat with Elevate Offsites?
A: Start by chatting with us here or leaving your email. Our events team will reach out within 24 hours to schedule a discovery call. From there, we handle venue selection, catering, logistics, and coordination - you just show up.

Q: How far in advance should I book?
A: We recommend at least 6 weeks for Urban packages, 8 weeks for Nature packages, and 12 weeks for Executive packages. Tighter timelines may be possible depending on availability - ask us.

Q: What is your cancellation policy?
A: Cancellations made 30+ days before the event receive a full refund minus a 10% administrative fee. Cancellations within 14-30 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 14 days are non-refundable. Rescheduling is free if done 21+ days in advance.

Q: What's included in each retreat package?
A: Each package includes venue rental, full catering, and a dedicated event coordinator. The Nature Package adds transportation and a guided activity. The Executive Package adds a private chef, premium bar, VIP transport, and a bespoke itinerary. See the package descriptions above for full details.

Q: Can I customize a package to fit our team's needs?
A: Yes. All packages can be customized with add-ons such as additional activities, extended stays, branded materials, or guest speakers. Our events team will work with you on a custom quote.

*Logistics*

Q: Do you arrange transportation to the venue?
A: The Nature Package and Executive Package include transportation. For the Urban Package, transportation is not included but can be arranged as an add-on. Our team will coordinate pickup points and schedules.

Q: Can you accommodate dietary restrictions and allergies?
A: Absolutely. All catering is fully customizable. We handle vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher, halal, and allergy-specific menus. Just let us know dietary needs during planning.

Q: Are your venues accessible for people with disabilities?
A: Yes. We only partner with venues that meet ADA accessibility standards. If you have specific accessibility requirements, let us know and we will confirm suitability before booking.

Q: What happens if there's bad weather during an outdoor retreat?
A: All Nature Package venues have indoor backup spaces. We also carry event insurance and have contingency plans for weather disruptions. Your event coordinator will have a Plan B ready.

*Pricing & Payment*

Q: What is the deposit requirement?
A: A 30% deposit is required to confirm your booking. The remaining balance is due 14 days before the event date.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept bank transfer (ACH), corporate credit cards, and wire transfer. Invoicing with NET-30 terms is available for companies with approved credit.

Q: Do you offer discounts for larger groups?
A: Our standard per-person pricing applies for groups up to 100. For groups larger than 100, we offer custom enterprise pricing - reach out to our events team for a tailored quote.

**WHAT WE DO NOT DO**
- We specialize in corporate events for groups of 15-200 people.
- We do not organize personal celebrations, weddings, or non-corporate events.
- We do not operate internationally (US venues only, as of 2026).
- We do not provide overnight accommodation booking directly, but our team can recommend and coordinate with nearby hotels.
